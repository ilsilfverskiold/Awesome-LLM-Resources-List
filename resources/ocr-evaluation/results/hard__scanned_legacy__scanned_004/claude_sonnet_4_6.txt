BACKGROUND AND PURPOSE

Following the original June 9th introduction in Denver and New Orleans, L.T. Brown was expanded into fields 4 and 9. Advertising and "down the street" activity began September 15. This topline report is based upon data which was collected eight weeks after the start of advertising.

The purpose of this research is to measure levels of awareness, trial, purchase and attitudes toward L.T. Brown in the expansion areas.

METHODOLOGY

A total of 602 random telephone interviews were conducted via WATS lines from a facility outside of Philadelphia, Pa. These interviews were among smokers located within fields 4 and 9. The sample was structured so that one half would represent field 4 and one half would represent field 9. Interviewing was confined to SMSA's within these fields and the number of interviews were skewed proportionately to the population of the SMSA's. In addition, the sample was equally divided between men and women aged 18-55 who smoke ten or more cigarettes per day. Interviewing took place between November 11 and November 25, 1975.
