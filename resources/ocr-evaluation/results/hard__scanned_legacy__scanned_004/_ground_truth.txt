MEMORANDUM December 23, 1975 TO: Mr. Russ Goyette FROM: William J. Doyle RE: L.T. BROWN AAU STUDY - FIELD 4 AND 9 WAVE I TOPLINE MRD #5546/3175 BACKGROUND AND PURPOSE Following the original June 9th introduction in Denver and New Orleans, L.T. Brown was expanded into fields 4 and 9. Advertising and "down the street" activity began September 15. This topline report is based upon data which was collected eight weeks after the start of advertising. The purpose of this research is to measure levels of awareness, trial, purchase and attitudes toward L.T. Brown in the expansion areas. METHODOL'OGY A total of 602 random telephone interviews were conducted via WATS lines from a facility outside of Philadelphia, Pa. These interviews were among smokers located within fields 4 and 9. The sample was structured so that one half would represent field 4 and one half would represent field 9. Interviewing was confined to SMSA's within these fields and the number of interviews were skewed proportionately to the population of the SMSA's. In addition, the sample was equally divided between men and women aged 18-55 who smoke ten or more cigarettes per day. Interviewing took place between November 11 and November 25, 1975. pgNbr=1 -2- SUMMARY I In fields 4 and 9 L.T. Brown achieves awareness levels similar to Wave II of Denver and New Orleans but directionally ahead of the Wave I performance in these two original test markets. Between fields 4 and 9, key measure scores are similar with the exception of advertising awareness and purchase, where a slight advantage goes to field 4. Compared to More, L.T. Brown is clearly behind on these aware- ness, trial and purchase measures. The levels achieved on these measures are similar to those achieved by Saratoga with the exception of brand awareness and purchase (particularly in field 9) where Saratoga has the advantage. Among 18-24 year old males and females awareness, trial and purchase of L.T. Brown are at lower levels than observed in Denver and New Orleans after 8 weeks out. Among 25-29 year old women these measures have increased very noticibly, especially advertising awareness and trial. Between men 30 and over in Denver and New Orleans (Wave I) and fields 4 and 9 not a great deal difference is noticed -- awareness is up while trial is slightly down.
